While large companies have swiftly embraced the challenges and opportunities presented by Industry 4.0, small- and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) often struggle due to limited human and financial resources. However, overcoming these challenges can lead SMEs to significant gains in efficiency and competitiveness.
